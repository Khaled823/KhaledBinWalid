**Role:** Grading Teaching Assistant
**Semester:** Fall 2025

Course covers design, operation, and control of modern electric power distribution systems including feeder modeling, fault detection (FLISR), Volt/Var Optimization, CVR technologies, and Smart Inverter/AMI applications. Supplemented with real-world industry case studies supporting grid modernization efforts.
